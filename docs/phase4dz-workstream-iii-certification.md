# Phase 4DZ — Workstream III Certification

Phase 4DZ is the fail-closed final gate for forecast and ranking optimization phases
4DA–4DY. Every phase must provide hash-protected completion evidence for exact forecast
equivalence, exact ranking equivalence, lineage, resource bounds, and offline/paper safety.
The gate also validates cumulative tests, Ruff, and a path allowlist limited to Workstream
III artifacts and the shared roadmap.

A passing result authorizes only progression to the next development workstream and
offline or paper evaluation. It never authorizes production deployment, connected trading,
or order execution. Unsafe paths, missing phases, failed proofs, malformed counts, schema
drift, or tampering fail closed. Publication is atomic and artifact-only.
