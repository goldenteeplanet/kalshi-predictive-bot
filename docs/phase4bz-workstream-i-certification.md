# Phase 4BZ — Workstream I Certification

Phase 4BZ certifies the latency-measurement and local-computation workstream from ordered,
hash-valid evidence covering the end-to-end baseline, critical-path DAG, latency budgets, offline
profiling, hotspot audit, and byte-equivalence proof. It also publishes explicit before/after
benchmark rows and residual uncertainty.

Certification requires every offline envelope to pass, byte-identical optimized outputs, unchanged
refusal order, complete evidence lineage, non-regressing benchmark measurements, and at least one
honest residual-uncertainty statement. A benchmark regression is reported as a non-certified state;
malformed, incomplete, tampered, or authorizing evidence fails closed. The artifact does not grant
trade authority or touch databases, services, networks, or exchanges.
